Hello everyone. I am not entirely sure why I have not done this sooner, but I finally want a proper place to document what I am working on: day-to-day adventures on the job, and a few threads that started back in university. The goal is partly selfish (I need to clarify my own thinking) and partly outward-facing (maybe some of it is useful to someone else wrestling with a similar problem).

I have always found it satisfying to write things down. My head is usually a bit of a mess of half-formed ideas, and putting them on screen gives them structure. That structure helps my actual work, and honestly, the writing process often surfaces ideas I would not have reached by just staring at a terminal.

---

## What you will find here (for now)

At the moment this blog is **technical by design**. That is where most of my energy goes, and where I still feel like I am actively paving a career path. Expect write-ups that look something like the posts already here:

*   Performance debugging on HPC systems (UCX, containers, interconnect quirks)
*   Measurement and profiling when the observer effect is the enemy
*   Pipelines and tooling from research and migration work
*   Course notes and tutorials I wanted to keep for myself

I try to write the way I debug: start from a concrete setup, report what surprised me, and name what I still do not know. When I can, I also try to connect the dots between posts: how a skill from one project shows up in another, how a lesson from HPC profiling relates to a pipeline problem months later. That is partly for my own sanity, but I hope it surfaces a more fundamental side of learning these things, not just a collection of one-off tricks.

---

## What might show up later

If I am being honest about long-term intent, technical posts are only half the picture. There is a whole other stack of questions I care about that my soon-to-be wife is politely less enthusiastic about at the dinner table: general anthropological puzzles, psychology, philosophy, the human condition, and the occasional ridiculous what-if or thought experiment that has been living rent-free in my head for years.

I do not know when that second lane opens up here. For now I am keeping the blog focused. But consider this a heads-up that the scope may widen once the technical backlog feels less urgent.
