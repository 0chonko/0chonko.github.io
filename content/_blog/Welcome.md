Hello everyone. I am not entirely sure why I have not done this sooner, but I finally want a proper place to document what I am working on: day-to-day adventures on the job, and a few threads that started back in university. The goal is partly selfish (I need to clarify my own thinking) and partly outward-facing (maybe some of it is useful to someone else wrestling with a similar problem).

I have always found it satisfying to write things down. My head is usually a bit of a mess of half-formed ideas, and putting them on screen gives them structure. That structure helps my actual work, and honestly, the writing process often surfaces ideas I would not have reached by just staring at a terminal.

---

## What you will find here (for now)

At the moment this blog is **technical by design**. That is where most of my energy goes, and where I still feel like I am actively paving a career path. My primary goal is to get closer to the metal and optimize for performance, whether that is edge or ultra-scale. Right now, AI labs sit closest to the kind of engineering I like (inference, distributed training, kernel work, fast architecture iteration, memory systems). Expect write-ups that look something like the posts already here:

*   Performance debugging on HPC systems (UCX, containers, interconnect quirks)
*   GPU kernels
*   Measurement and profiling, including architecture effects
*   Recreating research findings
*   Paper and paper-collection reading notes
*   Course notes and tutorials

The HPC posts are mainly from older work; I do not currently have access to infrastructure with SOTA interconnects.

I try to write the way I debug: start from a concrete setup, report what surprised me, and name what I still do not know. When I can, I also try to connect the dots between posts: how a skill from one project shows up in another, how a lesson from HPC profiling relates to a pipeline problem months later, or how an algorithm relates to the way LLMs structure and use memory. That is partly for my own overview (and trying to maximize that brain spatial locality), and I hope it surfaces a more fundamental side of learning these things, not just a collection of one-off tricks.

## What might show up later

If I am being honest about long-term intent, technical posts are only half the picture. There is a whole other stack of questions I care about that my soon-to-be wife is politely less enthusiastic about at the dinner table: general anthropological puzzles, psychology, philosophy, the human condition, and the occasional ridiculous what-if or thought experiment that has been living rent-free in my head for years. What if humanity managed resources from a global maximum of availability instead of local (state-limited) maxima? What if tech progress were capped and each stage artificially stretched so people could adapt gradually, so we could have a full decade of dedicated mp3/mp4 players, MSN, and messy variety in designs? And what would that do to monoculture and isomorphism?

I do not know when that second lane opens up here. For now I am keeping the blog focused. But consider this a heads-up that the scope may widen once the technical backlog feels less urgent.
